How to read this page

  • Facts only. Every row is a thing that was read or fetched, with where it came from and when. Nothing here is scored, rated, or judged, and nothing is colored good or bad.
  • An answer that's missing isn't a clean bill of health. "No answer" means the token didn't respond to that question, not that the thing doesn't exist. "Unavailable" means this page couldn't get the data, and the row says why.
  • The explorer can lag. The BOT Scan rows come from its index, not straight from the chain. The freshness row compares its latest block with the chain's.
  • Snapshots hold two kinds of fact. Some are read by the snapshot contract itself, so they can't be faked. Others are reported by whoever took the snapshot, and the contract can't check them. They're shown separately, with the reporter's address.
  • Look-alikes use two plain rules, stated in the row: the same symbol, or the same name once look-alike characters are normalized. A match is a fact about names, not a statement about the token.
